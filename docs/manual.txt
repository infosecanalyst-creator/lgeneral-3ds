== LGENERAL 3DS MANUAL
Turn-based hex strategy, played with the stylus.

Using this manual:
Circle Pad or C-Stick up / down: scroll the text (hold to keep scrolling).
D-pad: move between the chapter buttons on the touch screen.
L / R: previous / next chapter.   Tap a chapter to jump to it.
B or START: close.

Open it any time from the title screen or Start > Manual.
== 1. STARTING A GAME  (1/2)
The title screen:
Continue - resumes the autosave made at the start of your last turn.
New scenario - pick a set (K.u.k General WWI, or any other set you installed), then a battle. The top screen shows its briefing.
New campaign - a chain of battles; your core units carry over and results decide the next battle.
Load game - one of the ten save slots.
== 1. STARTING A GAME  (2/2)
Scenario setup, before you press Start:
Each side - tap to switch Human / CPU. Keep at least one side Human (you get a warning if not).
Fog of war - enemies are hidden until spotted.
Supply, Weather - realistic rules; leave on.
Deployment turn - place your units before turn 1.
Purchase - buy units with prestige.
== 2. THE TWO SCREENS
BOTTOM (touch): the battle map. You play everything here with the stylus.
TOP: your dashboard.
- status bar: turn, weather, side, prestige
- the selected unit's card
- the battle forecast before an attack
- the hex or enemy under your cursor
- messages and combat results
- minimap (white box = what you see) and objectives
== 3. STYLUS CONTROLS
Tap your unit - select it.
Tap a bright hex - move there.
Drag a unit - move it in one motion; drag it onto an enemy to see the forecast and let go to attack.
Tap an enemy - battle forecast on top; tap it again to attack.
Drag the empty map - pan.
Hold on any unit - full statistics.
Tap the selected unit - switch between the air and ground unit on that hex, or deselect.
Tap an enemy with nothing selected - where it can move (red) and attack (orange).
== 4. BUTTONS
Circle Pad / D-pad - move the hex cursor;  A - act on it.
B - cancel, deselect, back.    X - undo the last move.
Y - end turn (warns if units can still act).
R - next unit that still has orders.
Hold L - every enemy's threat range.
ZL - swap the screens: the map moves to the top screen and the dashboard to the touch screen (touch is off while swapped; play with the D-pad and A). ZL works at any time.
ZR - zoom: cycles 50%, 75%, 100%.
C-stick - pan.   Start - menu.   Select - overview map.
== 5. READING THE MAP  (1/2)
BLUE hex edge and number = your units (and allies).
RED hex edge and number  = enemy units you can see.
The number is the unit's strength (10 = full).
White top edge on a badge = an aircraft.
Yellow number = that unit is low on ammo or fuel.
Thick GOLD ring = the selected unit.
== 5. READING THE MAP  (2/2)
Green dot = unit can still move.
Amber dot = it has moved but can still attack.
No dot = finished for this turn.
With a unit selected, hexes it can reach stay bright; everything else is darkened.
Red tint = enemy can move there; orange = it can attack there (tap enemy, or hold L).
== 6. MOVING AND ATTACKING  (1/2)
Moving next to an enemy ends your movement for the turn (zone of control), so plan the last step.
Attack from a hex next to the enemy (artillery, ships and bombers reach further).
The first tap on an enemy shows the FORECAST: the expected losses for both sides. Tap again to attack, or tap elsewhere to cancel. Quicker: drag your unit onto the enemy and let go.
A unit can usually move then attack, or attack then stay put.
== 6. MOVING AND ATTACKING  (2/2)
Units that do not move dig in (entrenchment); dug-in units in towns, forests and hills are very hard to dislodge.
Your artillery, anti-tank and anti-air guns fire in support when an adjacent friend is attacked - so does the enemy's.
Hit hard units with artillery first, then attack.
Units gain experience (stars) in battle.
== 7. THE UNIT ACTION STRIP
Appears on the right when a unit is selected.
Supply - refill ammo and fuel. Uses the whole turn; works best near your cities and airfields and away from the enemy.
Undo - take back the last move (if no new enemy was spotted).
Air - embark into / out of air transport.
Merge - join two weak units of the same type.
Split - split off part of a unit's strength.
Info - full statistics (same as holding on a unit).
== 8. TURNS AND THE ENEMY TURN
End your turn with Y or Start > End turn.
R jumps to each unit that still has orders, so you never forget one; auto-select (Options) does it after each attack.
The enemy then moves (watch it, or switch "Watch the enemy turn" off in Options for speed).
Press Start during the enemy turn: the menu opens as soon as your turn begins.
== 9. PRESTIGE, BUYING, DEPLOYING
Prestige (top right) is earned by taking objectives and destroying enemy units.
Start > Purchase units: pick a class on the left, a unit, Transport if wanted, then Buy. X shows what you ordered (Refund to cancel).
New units arrive next turn: Start > Deploy units, tap one in the left strip, then a highlighted hex near your cities.
On a deployment turn you can also pick up placed units and move them.
== 10. UNIT TYPES  (1/2)
Infantry - holds cities, forests, hills. Slow.
Tanks - strong attack against soft and hard targets. Weak in towns and forests.
Recon - fast, sees far. Do not fight with it.
Anti-tank - strong against tanks; defends well.
Artillery - attacks from range; supports adjacent friends when they are attacked.
Anti-aircraft / Air defence - shoot down aircraft over and next to them.
== 10. UNIT TYPES  (2/2)
Fighters - kill enemy aircraft and escort bombers.
Tactical bombers - attack ground units.
Level bombers - damage cities and airfields, cutting enemy supply.
Ships - bombard the coast from range.
Ground transports carry infantry and guns faster; a unit in transport defends badly, so unload before contact.
Aircraft use fuel every turn: return to an airfield before it runs out.
== 11. SUPPLY, FUEL, WEATHER
Ammo is used by every attack; fuel by moving (and every turn for aircraft).
The Supply rate is higher near your cities and airfields and lower next to enemies.
Rain and snow slow ground units; bad weather limits aircraft. The status bar shows today's weather; Scenario info shows the forecast.
Rivers and mud are slow; roads are fast.
== 12. WINNING AND CAMPAIGNS
Objectives (top screen) are the victory hexes. Start > Victory conditions shows exactly what wins.
Taking every objective early usually gives a Major Victory; on time a Minor one; otherwise a defeat.
In a campaign, a Major Victory can open a better next battle (or bonus prestige); your core units keep their experience.
== 13. SAVING, OPTIONS, FILES
Autosave at the start of each of your turns (Continue on the title screen). Start > Save game: ten slots.
Options: forecast tap, auto-select, unit badges, hex grid, watch enemy turn, animation speed, sound, and Left hand (swaps the D-pad with Y, A, B and X: Y is up, A is right, B is down, X is left, and the reverse; for holding the stylus in the left hand; remembered).
Files live in sdmc:/3ds/lgeneral/. If something goes wrong, lgeneral.log and crash.txt there show what happened.
Install or update over Wi-Fi with ftpd.
== 14. ENCYCLOPEDIA
Start > Encyclopedia: every unit and terrain type, with the numbers that matter.
UNITS: pick a class on the left (or L / R), then a unit. The top screen shows its cost, years in service, movement, initiative, spotting, range, attack against each target type, defenses, special abilities and how to use it. Nation cycles the countries.
TERRAIN: movement cost for each movement type, how deep units dig in, initiative limits and supply - for any weather (L / R).
From the Unit list, hold a unit (or tap Encyclopedia) to open its page.
== 15. STRATEGY TIPS
- Soften a strong position with artillery or bombers before the main attack.
- Keep fighters over your bombers.
- Put infantry in towns and forests; keep tanks in the open.
- Never end a turn with a transport next to the enemy.
- Watch the turn limit: take the objectives, not every enemy unit.
- Use Supply before low ammo becomes no ammo.
